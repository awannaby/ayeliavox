## What is AYELIAVOX?

AYELIAVOX Podcast Studio turns your texts into multi-voice podcasts on your own Windows PC or Apple Silicon Mac (M1 and later).

- **Multi-speaker**: up to 10 voices in one production
- **Multilingual**: one script, many languages
- **Multi-engine**: Piper, Kokoro, Chatterbox, Qwen3-TTS locally; Microsoft / Azure optional with your own key
- **Voice cloning in 30 seconds**: only with the rights of the person cloned
- **No cloud, no subscription**: lifetime license, free edition available

> Voice cloning: the rights of the person cloned are required, under your sole responsibility. Chatterbox and Qwen3-TTS require an NVIDIA RTX GPU on Windows.
