QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Is the Free edition really free?

Yes. It is free for life, with no credit card, no credits and no time limit. It includes unlimited audio generation, 1 voice per production and 1 personal cloned voice.

Does it work without the cloud?

The local engines (Piper, Kokoro, Chatterbox and Qwen3-TTS) produce audio on your computer. Microsoft / Azure is an optional cloud engine that you enable with your own API key.

Is there a subscription?

No. Paid editions and modules are one-time lifetime licenses, and the Free edition has no fee.

Which computers are supported?

Windows PCs and Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later). On Windows, Chatterbox and Qwen3-TTS require an NVIDIA RTX GPU.

Can I clone any voice?

Only your own voice, or a voice you are authorized to use. Cloning remains your responsibility.
